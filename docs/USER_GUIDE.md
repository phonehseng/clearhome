## Find housing

1. Open `/app` and describe your situation or use the form. Give a location and a realistic monthly budget. Add household, bedroom, accessibility, age, or timing details when useful.
2. Review both affordable and regular options. An income-restricted property may have income limits or a waitlist; a regular rental is not automatically subsidized.
3. Use the housing browser to narrow the list. Available filters distinguish geography, category, age eligibility, rooms versus whole homes, budget, and other supported attributes.
4. Expand **Eligibility & important details** and floor-plan options. A community card can represent several opportunities with different prices or restrictions.
5. Open the provider/source link or call the listed office to confirm costs, eligibility, move-in timing, deposits, utilities, availability, and how to apply.

Northern Virginia covers Arlington, Fairfax, Loudoun, and Prince William counties and the independent cities in the catalog. Blacksburg is a separate service area with student-room options and New River Valley support resources. A listing for one region should not be treated as local to the other.

## Read prices and availability carefully

- A **room price** is generally per person; a **whole-home price** covers the home. Read the explicit rent basis.
- An advertised **installment** is not necessarily a calendar-month rent. Check the term, payment schedule, move-in dates, and total cost with the provider.
- Unknown prices are not treated as zero and are excluded by numeric budget filters.
- A starting rent does not include every possible fee or establish your eligibility.
- An open waitlist is permission to join a queue, not an immediate vacancy or placement guarantee.
- Contact-for-availability and leasing labels do not reserve a unit. Conflicting or unconfirmed move-in dates remain caveats.
- Review dates describe evidence checks, not real-time inventory. Stale records may disappear until rechecked.
- Roommates may have individual agreements or shared responsibility for one lease. Confirm this before paying or signing.

Some chat paths display explicitly labeled rent estimates based on published figures or comparable reviewed homes. These are not provider quotes. Only provider-confirmed costs should inform an application decision.

## Use chat and maps

The assistant uses reviewed records for recommendations and may return matching resources even when the AI service is unavailable. Answers arrive in parts through streaming chat. Keep sensitive identifiers out of your question.

For a nearby-place question, describe the location clearly, such as a workplace address or landmark. Some answers use distance-based estimates when route data is unavailable; those estimates are labeled and are not verified walking times.

The dedicated walking search checks a bounded set of matching homes near a supermarket, pharmacy, or transit stop. A strict walking limit needs a qualifying route result. A preference can retain options whose walking time is unavailable or exceeds the target. It does not search every property in the region. Missing coordinates, provider/API failures, and unconfigured keys can limit results. Follow the route and source links to confirm suitability.

Live web results are labeled **not checked by the team**. They are discovery leads, not approved catalog recommendations. Verify the provider and source before sharing documents or sending money.

## Photos, saved listings, and sharing

Click a property photo's enlarge control for a larger view; desktop pointer hover also previews it. Keyboard users can focus the button and press Enter, then Escape to close. Provider renderings are labeled. Model apartments, older community photos, and renderings may differ from the unit being offered.

Open the source link to inspect the publisher. A licensed archive photo includes photographer/source/license credits. If delivery fails, use the source link; an unavailable photo does not establish that the housing itself is unavailable.

Save listings with the card's Save control. Favorites are stored in this browser's local storage, without an account or cross-device sync. Clearing site data can remove them; saved information may become stale. Sharing copies a listing URL that another person can open. Saving or sharing does not hold an apartment or notify a landlord.

Buttons, navigation, and decorative images resist accidental highlighting. Useful listing text, addresses, prices, and form content remain copyable. The current design uses a light color scheme; it does not automatically switch to device dark mode.

## Emergency help and applications

For immediate danger call **911**. For help locating shelter or emergency housing resources call **211** and contact the listed local service directly. Clear Home cannot guarantee a shelter bed, place someone in housing, or dispatch emergency services.

The current website has no lease upload, lease analysis, signing wizard, document forwarding, or electronic-signature service. Apply and sign through the verified provider. General housing explanations are informational and are not legal advice; use a listed legal-aid organization for advice about your situation.
