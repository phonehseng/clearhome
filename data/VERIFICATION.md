## Uncertainties retained

- Several owner directories and property sites use different leasing/tracking phone numbers. Records use the identified provider-site contact and note conflicts where relevant. No phone number has been tested by calling.
- Witter Place has conflicting street-address descriptions and outdated construction language despite newer opening news. Its closed application status is consistent across owner and city, so it remains excluded.
- No accessible vacancy was explicitly established; `accessible` remains unknown even where a community describes accessible apartments. No emergency bed or guaranteed same-day placement was established.
- Prices are floor-plan snapshots; advertised counts may include future dates, and income restrictions can vary by apartment. Do not sum these records into a guaranteed number of vacant homes.
- Review again by **October 31, 2026**. The backend excludes overdue availability records rather than silently retaining them as fresh matches. This is a first research batch; it is not a complete census of housing across Northern Virginia.

## Community browsing and budget expansion — October 24, 2026

The browser now shows one card per physical community, with separate floor-plan prices,
requirements, status evidence, and sources inside the card. Plans are filtered before
being grouped: a $1,500 budget cannot pull a more expensive plan back into a result.
Distinct AMI tiers remain separate options. Matching imported options are identified by
normalized address, jurisdiction, floor plan, bedroom/bathroom counts, category/program,
and record kind. The newest reviewed snapshot wins; a newer closure is retained rather
than reviving an older advertisement. The validator rejects duplicate housing options
with different IDs. Separate housing-authority programs are not combined merely because
they share an office.
